Founded in 1990, the same year Namibia gained independence — Porky's Meat Market has grown alongside a young nation, serving households and institutions for three and a half decades.

// 1990 — THE BEGINNING

FROM A MODEST
COUNTER TO A
NATIONAL SUPPLIER.

When Namibia gained independence in 1990, a small butchery named Porky's Meat Market opened its doors for the first time. What began as a modest family business — serving households a few cuts at a time — has grown into one of the country's most recognised meat brands.

Today, we operate from a dedicated production facility on Rendsburger Street in the Lafrenz Industrial Area, supplying everything from neighbourhood spazas to government institutions across all 14 regions.

"Freshness starts here."
